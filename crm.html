Our Own Team CRM

One system for everything our real estate team needs, built in-house around how our agents actually work.

LeadsUnder contractInspectionClosing

The spark

How it started

Our team was paying more than $3,000 a month for two platforms: one for online leads and one for our CRM. And the leads weren’t the kind that turn into real relationships.

So I asked: how could we do this differently? What if I built us our own?

The build

What I’m building

One system for everything we need, so nothing lives in five different places:

  • A Trello-style deal board, so we can retire Trello
  • Buyer and seller checklist templates that drop onto each deal
  • Notes on every deal
  • Contacts with family members and multiple addresses
  • Links straight to each deal’s files
  • Our leads, tracked in the same place as everything else

Next up: a daily dashboard for our morning meetings, and automating the documents I build and send to other parties in a deal.

The why

Why it matters

Everything we need lives in one place, and the money we save can go where we believe it matters most: our community and our network.

We’re choosing quality relationships over cold-call lead counts.

The process

How I’m building it

I define what the team needs and design how it works, and Claude helps me build it as real, hosted code. Each round of feedback from our agents shapes the next version.
